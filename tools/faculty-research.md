## Flags: lines on the page that look outdated or wrong

1. **Kunal Shah, "Founder, CRED"**: outdated. Since June 2026 he leads WhatsApp at Meta and has stepped away from CRED operations (interim CEO: Miten Sampat). He remains founder and shareholder. Update to "Head of WhatsApp · Founder, CRED".
2. **Sidhant Goyal, "Toddle, Ex-Sequoia Capital"**: outdated. He was VP Growth at Toddle Jan 2024 to Jan 2026 and is now Principal at Crane Venture Partners (India).
3. **Amit Puniyani, "Ex-VP, Deutsche Bank / J.P. Morgan"**: partly unsupported. JAGSoM lists him as "Ex VP - Deutsche Bank, Credit Suisse". J.P. Morgan appears only in a data-aggregator record as a quantitative analyst role, not VP. Swap J.P. Morgan for Credit Suisse.
4. **Deepinder Goyal, "Co-founder, Zomato"**: not wrong, but sources call him the founder; he resigned as Eternal MD & CEO effective 1 Feb 2026 and is Vice Chairman. Consider "Founder, Zomato (Eternal)".
5. **Jacob Singh, "Ex-CTO, Blinkit (Grofers)"**: accurate, but he left Blinkit in 2020; since July 2025 he co-founded Sing One Song (AI singing coach). Fine to keep the "Ex" line.
6. **Gaurav Dadhich, "Founder"** and **Vatsal Sanghvi, "Founder"**: the company is missing. Gaurav: Maximem AI. Vatsal: 1811 Labs.
7. **Binny Bansal, "Co-founder, Flipkart"**: fine as a legacy line. He exited the Flipkart board in Jan 2024 (and PhonePe's board in Nov 2024); current venture is Opptra (launched as OppDoor).
8. **Siddharth Menon**: most sources (LinkedIn, ad:tech, exchange4media, afaqs) spell it "Siddarth". HiveSchool uses "Siddharth". Confirm with him.
9. **Kanishk Mehta**: other SSB pages call him "Operations Manager" (origin.ssb.scaler.com) or "Product Head". The "Sr. Product Director, Razorpay" line on this page is the one backed by sources.
10. **Puneet**: surname is **Gupta**. SSB's 2024 brochure lists "Puneet Gupta, Principal, BCG"; he became Partner in Jan 2025 and is now based in Toronto per LinkedIn.
